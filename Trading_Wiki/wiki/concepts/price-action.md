## Definition

Reading the raw behaviour of price — bar/candle shape, position of the close, wicks, range, and the sequence of swings — without relying on lagging indicators, to infer which party is in control and where the market is likely to go next.

## How different sources approach it

- **Understanding Price Action (Volman):** Seven principles (double pressure, S/R, false/tease/proper breaks, false highs/lows, pullback reversals, ceiling test, round-number effect) on the 5-minute chart. Entry is the break of a crucial bar after mandatory buildup; everything is read from price structure and tension.
- **Trades About to Happen (Weis):** Effort vs reward (volume vs price spread), ease of movement, position of the close, and shortening of thrust are price-action tools applied to bar charts across all timeframes.
- **VPA (Coulling):** The three premier candles — shooting star, hammer, doji — gain meaning only with volume. Price alone tells half the story; conviction comes from the volume bar. Art, not science.
- **Crypto Technical Analysis (John & Law):** Candlestick patterns (single and multi-candle) form the backbone of crypto TA because the 24/7 market generates clean, uninterrupted sequences; higher timeframes (4h, daily, weekly) and large-cap coins produce fewer false patterns.
- **Price Action Institutional:** Institutional footprints — sideways accumulation, aggressive initiation candles, strong rejections — read directly from price to detect where large participants acted.
- **Options Playbook (Overby):** Volman price-action principles are explicitly recommended as the entry-timing layer for options trades; the strategy defines the risk, price action defines the trigger.

## Cross-domain insights

Price action is the lowest-common-denominator skill — it needs only a chart and works on every instrument and timeframe. It is also the entry layer that options traders bolt onto strategy selection: the Greeks define the bet, but price action pulls the trigger. Where indicators lag, price action reads the present.
