## Definition

Price levels where buying (support) or selling (resistance) has repeatedly overcome the opposing force, creating boundaries within a trading range. Former support tends to become resistance once broken and vice versa (role reversal).

## How different sources approach it

- **Trades About to Happen (Weis):** Lines frame the struggle between buyers and sellers; the interaction of price with previously drawn lines is more significant than the lines themselves. Axis lines around which prices revolve are especially informative.
- **VPA (Coulling):** Volume validates whether a level is genuine — support tested on low volume likely holds; high volume at support signals absorption and a potential breakdown. Dynamic volume-weighted trend lines reveal where "smart money" drew its lines earlier than traditional lines.
- **Volume Profile (Dale / Villahermosa):** High Volume Nodes become support/resistance; Low Volume Nodes act as rejection zones. Value Area edges (VAH/VAL) are dynamic S/R the market defends.
- **Crypto Technical Analysis (John & Law):** Support/resistance hold "more dramatically" in crypto due to lower liquidity and concentration of orders at psychologically significant round numbers ($10k, $50k for Bitcoin).
- **Understanding Price Action (Volman):** S/R reveals the dominant party; "we shouldn't trade against dominance." Conservative practice is to monitor behaviour *in and around* levels before acting, not to fade them blindly.

## Cross-domain insights

Support and resistance is the most portable concept in trading: identical role-reversal logic works on a 5-minute forex chart, a daily equity chart, a Bitcoin daily, and an options breakeven. What changes across domains is how levels are validated — volume in equities, psychological round numbers in crypto, buildup tension in scalping.
