## Definition

The collective emotional state of market participants — fear versus greed, euphoria versus panic — which tends to reach extremes precisely at major turning points. Sentiment analysis measures and fades these extremes.

## How different sources approach it

- **Trading Against the Crowd (Summa):** Put/call ratios, VIX/IV extremes, short-selling ratios, and advisory bull/bear ratios quantify the crowd. The equity-only put/call is the most reliable contrarian signal; Squeeze Play systems require a price trigger to confirm the sentiment extreme.
- **Crypto Hype Analysis (John & Law):** Fear & Greed Index, social media sentiment (Twitter/Reddit/Discord), Google Trends, and Bitcoin dominance all serve as sentiment barometers; extreme greed signals a potential sell.
- **Options Volatility (Overby):** The VIX "fear gauge" stays range-bound for months; spikes above range mark pessimism bottoms, collapses below range mark complacency tops. IV mean-reverts around the emotional average.
- **Understanding Price Action (Volman):** The round-number effect — 00 and 50 levels act as psychological magnets where price revolves for hours; in low volatility attention shifts to 20-levels. Crowd behaviour at these magnets creates both opportunity and trap.
- **VPA (Coulling):** Buying climax at tops is "euphoric public buying meets insiders distributing"; selling climax at bottoms is "panic selling meets aggressive insiders." Emotion is the fuel; volume is the evidence.

## Cross-domain insights

Sentiment is the same beast in every cage: frustrated crowds at round-number magnets (scalping), euphoric put/call extremes (options), fear-greed crypto indices, and VIX spikes (equities). Each domain developed its own thermometer, but all converge on the contrarian edge — when the crowd is most certain, the reversal is nearest.
