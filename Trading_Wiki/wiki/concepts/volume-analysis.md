## Definition

The study of traded volume — how much, where, and when — to validate or invalidate price action. Volume is the only real-time, leading measure of market participation; it cannot be hidden because every transaction is recorded.

## How different sources approach it

- **Wyckoff 2.0 (Villahermosa):** Volume Profile displays volume horizontally by price level, exposing the Point of Control (POC), value areas, and High/Low Volume Nodes that mark zones of acceptance and rejection.
- **Volume Profile (Trader Dale):** Profile shapes (D, P, b, Thin) reveal market behaviour; setups (accumulation, trend, rejection, reversal) are built on where volume accumulates versus where price finds no acceptance.
- **VPA (Coulling):** Reduces to one binary judgement per bar — confirmation (volume agrees with price) versus anomaly (volume disagrees). Volume distinguishes genuine moves from traps.
- **Crypto Technical Analysis (John & Law):** Volume is the most critical confirming indicator; a breakout on low volume is a "head fake." MFI (volume-weighted RSI) outperforms RSI alone.
- **Understanding Price Action (Volman):** Buildup at a level concentrates volume in a tight cluster; the quality of that pre-breakout tension determines stop placement and trade quality.

## Cross-domain insights

Volume is the universal truth-teller. In equities it confirms breakouts, in crypto it filters false signals from high volatility, in options it underpins implied-volatility sentiment, and in scalping it flags the buildup that precedes explosive breaks. Every domain converges on one rule: a move without volume agreement is suspect.
