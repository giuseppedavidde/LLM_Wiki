## Definition

The phases in which "smart money" (insiders, institutions) quietly builds (accumulation) or unwinds (distribution) a position while the public is on the wrong side. Accumulation precedes uptrends; distribution precedes downtrends. The cause (the phase) determines the effect (the resulting trend).

## How different sources approach it

- **VPA (Coulling):** Three-step micro/macro/global process maps to the cycle — high volume on down moves that fail to follow through (accumulation), high volume on up moves that stall (distribution), climax as the explosive "firework display" ending each phase. Stopping volume and topping-out volume are the footprints.
- **Wyckoff 2.0 (Villahermosa):** The Law of Cause and Effect — duration and extent of accumulation/distribution determine the magnitude of the trend. The analytical challenge is distinguishing genuine accumulation from a failed distribution; sloping structures behave as accumulation/distribution zones even when not horizontal.
- **Trades About to Happen (Weis):** Reframes absorption dynamics rather than rigid labelling — the price/volume clues reveal the immediate balance of power regardless of the broader phase. Wyckoff's "bag-holding" — persistent selling failing to weaken price — precedes a rally.
- **Crypto Fundamental Analysis (John & Law):** Exchange flows proxy accumulation/distribution — deposits signal selling intent, withdrawals signal accumulation. Miner deposits to exchanges flag impending distribution; rising active addresses plus rising price confirm organic accumulation.
- **Order Flow (Villahermosa):** Buying/selling absorption at HVNs is the micro-structure of accumulation/distribution; initiative buying attacking a passive-selling ASK is the signature turn.

## Cross-domain insights

Accumulation/distribution is the master narrative behind every other concept: springs and upthrusts are its turning points, support/resistance are its boundaries, volume anomalies are its fingerprints. Crypto translates the same idea into on-chain exchange flows, while equity VPA reads it from candle-volume. The insiders-versus-public dynamic is invariant; only the data channel differs.
