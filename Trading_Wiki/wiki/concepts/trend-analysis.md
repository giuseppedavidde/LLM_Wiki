## Definition

Identification of the prevailing directional bias of the market — higher highs/lows (uptrend), lower highs/lows (downtrend), or balance (range) — and the practice of aligning trades *with* rather than against that dominance. One of TA's three core premises: "prices move in trends."

## How different sources approach it

- **Wyckoff Method (Weis/Villahermosa):** The Law of Supply and Demand drives all movement; once dominance is established it takes time to roll over, so "we shouldn't trade against dominance." Slope of the market, higher highs, and corrections that fail to surpass former lows define the trend. Shortening of Thrust (SOT) signals waning momentum within the trend.
- **Understanding Price Action (Volman):** The most effective way to identify dominance is the overall slope; the stronger the earlier dominance, the less likely a first reversal attempt will succeed. A false break *in line* with dominance is a temporary correction; one *against* it signals a power shift.
- **Crypto Technical Analysis (John & Law):** Moving averages (especially 50- and 200-day) serve as dynamic support/resistance; golden/death crosses generate signals. In strong crypto trends, RSI can remain overbought for extended periods, reducing oscillator reliability — trend trumps oscillators.
- **VPA (Coulling):** Trends follow accumulation→markup and distribution→markdown cycles; volume confirms the trend (rising price + rising volume = strong) and anomalies (rising price + falling volume = weak rally) flag exhaustion.
- **Options Playbook (Overby):** Strategy selection begins with market outlook (bullish/bearish/neutral/volatile); directional strategies (long call/put, spreads) require a clear trend thesis, while delta defines the directional exposure of every position.

## Cross-domain insights

Trend is the frame on which every other concept hangs. Scalpers respect dominance before fading a level, crypto traders override oscillators with moving averages, VPA reads volume to confirm trend health, and options traders choose the entire strategy family from the trend verdict. Across domains the cardinal rule repeats: trade with the trend, or wait.
