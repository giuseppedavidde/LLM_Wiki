## What this book covers
Integrated framework that fuses the classical Wyckoff Method with modern Volume Profile and Order Flow analysis. It re-establishes Wyckoff's three laws (supply/demand, cause/effect, effort/result) and his structural labels (PS, SC, AR, ST, Spring, UTAD, LPS, SOW) in the context of horizontal volume distribution and footprint reading. The central thesis is that combining the *logic* of Wyckoff with the *objectivity* of volume profile and order flow yields higher-confidence trade scenarios.
