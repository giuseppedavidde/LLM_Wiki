## What this book covers
A workbook of step-by-step exercises and self-tests complementing the Options Course. It reinforces fundamentals (moneyness, intrinsic vs time value, exercise/assignment), the Greeks, volatility, and the construction of covered/income, vertical-spread, volatility, calendar and advanced strategies through worked problems and quiz questions.
