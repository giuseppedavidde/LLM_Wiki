## What this book covers
A practical, insider-oriented guide to horizontal volume analysis. Trader Dale explains how to read the Volume Profile tool, identify the Point of Control (POC) and Value Area (VAH/VAL), and trade using profile shapes (D, P, b, Thin) and high/low volume nodes. The book is heavy on concrete setups: accumulation, trend continuation, rejection, and reversal trades around the POC.
