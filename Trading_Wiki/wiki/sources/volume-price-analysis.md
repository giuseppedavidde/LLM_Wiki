## What this book covers
A streamlined methodology that uses volume to validate or invalidate price action. Anna Coulling distils the principles inherited from Charles Dow, Jesse Livermore, and Richard Wyckoff into a single question — *does volume confirm the price move, or reveal an anomaly?* The book covers the three premier VPA candles, stopping volume, climax, and the difference between confirmation and anomaly across all timeframes.
