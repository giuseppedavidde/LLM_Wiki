## What this book covers
A modern revival of Wyckoff's tape reading, framed around clusters, displacement, and the Law of Supply and Demand. David Weis introduces the Weis Wave — a cumulative wave that nets price swings and volume to expose the effort behind each move — and the "Sign of Trend" (SOT) as a momentum-divergence tool. The book shows how to anticipate institutional entry and exit through the order flow left on the tape.
