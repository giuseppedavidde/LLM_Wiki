## What this book covers
A compact beginner-to-intermediate crash course on options, covering contract specs, moneyness, intrinsic vs time value, open interest, exercise/assignment, covered vs naked positions, the Greeks, HV/IV, the volatility crush, and a curated set of income and spread strategies. It also supplies cross-domain bridges — volatility links to contrarian sentiment and crypto, and price-action links to scalping.
