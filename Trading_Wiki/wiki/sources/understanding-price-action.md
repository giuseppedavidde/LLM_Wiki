## What this book covers
A scalping methodology for the 5-minute chart, taught entirely through documented chart examples. Volman defines seven core principles (double pressure, S/R, false/tease/proper breaks, false highs/lows, pullback reversals, ceiling test, round-number effect) and four trade setups (pattern break, pattern break pullback, pattern break combi, pullback reversal) with fixed stops and targets, plus manual-exit techniques and a low-volatility adaptation.

## Key concepts introduced
- Double pressure: equal commitment of bid and ask
- False / tease / proper breaks of support and resistance
- False highs/lows and pullback-reversal logic
- Pattern-break setup, pullback, combi, and pullback-reversal trade templates
- Manual exits: news report exit, resistance exit, reversal exit
- Low-volatility adaptation (tighter bracket, 200-tick frame, 20-levels)
