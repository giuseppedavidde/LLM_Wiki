## What this book covers
A reference of 40+ options strategies organized by market outlook (bullish, bearish, neutral, volatile, low-volatility) and complexity (rookie to all-star). For each play it explains construction, rationale, max profit/loss, breakeven, Greeks, and entry/exit rules. Includes the strategy-selection framework and a primer on IV, the VIX, and volatility skew.
