## What this book covers
A contrarian framework for using sentiment indicators as fade signals. Summa details how to trade against extreme positioning via the put/call ratio (equity-only, total, OEX), the Squeeze Play I & II systems, the Tsunami Sentiment Wave, implied-volatility extremes, short-selling ratios, and advisory/media sentiment. The thesis: when sentiment reaches one-sided extremes, the crowd is usually wrong.
