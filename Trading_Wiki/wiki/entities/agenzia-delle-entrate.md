## Role in trading knowledge
The Agenzia delle Entrate defines the Italian tax framework for derivatives, capital gains, and cryptoassets. Its instructions (Quadro RT, RW, IVAFE, imposta sostitutiva) govern how Italian residents report and pay tax on the instruments covered throughout this wiki — the regulatory ceiling over all trading activity in the Italian jurisdiction.
