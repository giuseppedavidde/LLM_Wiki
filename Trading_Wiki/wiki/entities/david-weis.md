## Role in trading knowledge
Weis revived Wyckoff's tape reading for electronic markets, inventing the cumulative Weis Wave (price + volume) and the Sign of Trend (SOT) divergence tool. His "Trades About to Happen" is the canonical link between classical tape reading and modern order-flow analysis.
