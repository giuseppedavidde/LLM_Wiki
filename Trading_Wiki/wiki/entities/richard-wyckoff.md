## Role in trading knowledge
Wyckoff is the originating figure of the entire trading-technical-analysis lineage in this wiki. His three laws (supply/demand, cause/effect, effort/result) and accumulation/distribution schematics ground every modern adaptation — Weis Wave, VPA, Volume Profile and Order Flow build directly on his work.
