## Role in trading knowledge
Coulling distilled the work of Dow, Livermore, and Wyckoff into a single confirm-or-anomaly decision rule based on volume. Her VPA framework is treated in this wiki as the third modern adaptation of the Wyckoff Method, joining Weis and Villahermosa.
