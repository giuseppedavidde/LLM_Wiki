# Wyckoff 2.0 Framework

> Sources: Rubén Villahermosa Chaves, 2021; David Weis, 2013
> Raw: [Wyckoff 2.0 Structures](../../raw/trading/wyckoff-2-0-structures-volume-profile-and-order-flow-combining-the-logic-of-the-.md); [Trades About to Happen](../../raw/trading/trades-about-to-happen-2013.md)

## Overview

Wyckoff 2.0 is the integrated framework proposed by Rubén Villahermosa Chaves, combining three pillars: the logic of the Wyckoff Methodology, the objectivity of Volume Profile, and the granularity of Order Flow analysis. The goal is to propose the most robust trading scenarios possible.

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Wyckoff Methodology (The Cornerstone)
- Provides the underlying logic and theoretical framework (Three Laws, accumulation/distribution)
- Offers context for scenario development
- Provides analytical tools to evaluate who is in control

The Law of Supply and Demand is the true engine of financial markets. The accumulation/distribution processes, combined with the Law of Cause and Effect, explain how trends develop.

### 2. Volume Profile (Objectivity)
- Objectively identifies zones of acceptance (HVN) and rejection (LVN)
- Removes subjectivity from support/resistance identification
- Provides precise levels for stop placement and targets

### 3. Order Flow (Granularity)
- Confirms intentions at key turning points
- Reveals institutional activity through imbalances
- Used only at key trading zones (not for continuous analysis)

## Context Analysis

### Trading Range Context
- Price is in balance within value areas
- Trade mean reversion from value area boundaries
- Look for springs and upthrusts at the edges

### Trend Context
- Price is in imbalance, moving away from value
- Look for continuation patterns
- Use VPOC migration to gauge trend health
- Trade pullbacks to value area

## Scenario Planning

1. **Identify the context** — is price in a trading range or in a trend?
2. **Mark key zones** — HVNs, LVNs, POCs, support/resistance
3. **Define scenarios** — what would confirm bullish vs. bearish?
4. **Use Order Flow at trigger zones** — confirm with footprint imbalances
5. **Manage risk** — use value areas for stops and targets

## Trading Strategy

### In a Trading Range
- Short near VAH with bearish order flow
- Long near VAL with bullish order flow
- Stop beyond the value area extreme
- Target the opposite value area boundary

### In an Uptrend
- Long on pullbacks to value area with bullish order flow
- Short only if trend structure breaks and order flow confirms

### In a Downtrend
- Short on rallies to value area with bearish order flow
- Long only if trend structure breaks and order flow confirms

## Key Insight

The market must be given *flexibility* in the development of structures. The Wyckoff methodology is not about robotically labeling every move — it is about objectively analyzing what the market does and fails to do to determine who is in control.
