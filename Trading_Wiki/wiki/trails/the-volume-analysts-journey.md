## Path

A single thread — volume as the truth-teller — followed from the raw bar down to the order book and out into crypto and scalping, exposing the one epistemological fault line every volume analyst must cross.

1. Start with: [[bar-chart-reading|How a bar is read — price range, close position, and volume as the foundational triplet]]
2. Connect to: [[volume-analysis|The cross-cutting concept — volume as the universal real-time, leading measure]]
3. Then understand: [[volume-profile|The objective map — POC, value areas, HVN/LVN as measurable horizontal volume]]
4. Then understand: [[volume-price-analysis-vpa|The interpretive art — confirmation vs anomaly as narrative reading]]
5. Challenge with: [[volume-profile-objectivity-vs-vpa-interpretive-art|The epistemological contradiction — does the edge live in the number or the story?]]
6. Descend into: [[order-flow-footprint|Micro-structure — delta, imbalances, and absorption behind the candle]]
7. End at: [[crypto-technical-analysis|Crypto — where volume confirms breakouts against high-frequency head fakes]]

## Why this trail

Every book in this wiki invokes volume, but they split into two epistemologies: the measurable ([[volume-profile|Dale]]) and the felt ([[volume-price-analysis-vpa|Coulling]]). The trail stages the disagreement on purpose at step 5 so the reader reaches the order-flow and crypto stops already aware that "volume confirms" is never self-evident — it is always a choice between reading a number and reading a story. The crypto terminus is deliberate: in the thinnest books, the cost of choosing the wrong epistemology is the highest, which is why the same confirmation rule that is good advice in equities becomes survival in crypto.
