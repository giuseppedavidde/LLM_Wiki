## Why this trail

The spring is the bridge: at the chart it is a failed breakdown reversed by absorption; at the options desk it is the volatility-compression event whose resolution a long straddle or call spread monetises. Walking the path exposes one recurring insight — **the trade idea is invariant; only the instrument and the risk-measurement language change.** Volman traders read the snap; options traders price the snap. The contradiction stop forces the reader to decide which vocabulary to trust, and the final stop shows how to use both without confusing them.
