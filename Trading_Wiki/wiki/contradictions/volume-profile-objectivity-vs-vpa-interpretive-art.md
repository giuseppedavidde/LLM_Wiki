## The Tension

Both Volume Profile and Volume Price Analysis claim descent from Wyckoff and treat
volume as the "truth behind price." Yet they assert opposite epistemologies. Volume
Profile markets itself as "totally objective — it does not require interpretation,"
reducing the chart to measurable POC, VAH, VAL, and node shapes. VPA insists it is
"Art, Not Science," that "software cannot replicate the contextual analysis," and
demands subjective judgment of every candle against its volume history.

## Position A — Objective Measurement (Volume Profile)

The Volume Profile entry states unambiguously that the tool "does not require
interpretation." Profiles are classified by **observable shape** (D, P, b, thin),
value areas are mechanically defined (70% of volume), and trades follow fixed
principles: reversion inside value, continuation on acceptance out of value, failed
reversion as trend confirmation. Trader Dale's setups — **Volume Accumulation**,
**Trend**, **Rejection** — are rule-based triggers off histogram levels. The claim is
that horizontal volume removes the analyst's bias: *where* volume occurred is a fact.

## Position B — Interpretive Art (VPA / Coulling)

Coulling's VPA is built on the opposite premise. Its first guiding principle: **"Art,
Not Science — VPA requires subjective judgment. Software cannot replicate the
contextual analysis of comparing price behaviour against volume history."** Every bar
is a binary *confirmation vs. anomaly* judgment, but the judgment itself depends on
context — is this Shooting Star at a trend high with above-average volume, or a pause
with below-average? The "Three Premier Candles" (Shooting Star, Hammer, Doji) only
gain meaning through the analyst's reading of accumulation/distribution cycle and
follow-through. Coulling rejects the notion that volume can be read off a histogram
without contextual narrative.

## Resolution / Synthesis

The contradiction is partly **false opposition**. Even Volume Profile "objective"
setups require the trader to choose *which* session profile, *which* value area, and
whether a rejection is genuine — judgments Villahermosa admits when he says the market
"must be given flexibility" and that labels "are guidelines, not rigid rules."
Conversely VPA's anomalies are anchored to measurable volume (above/below average,
climactic). The real synthesis: **Volume Profile supplies the objective map (levels,
nodes, value areas); VPA supplies the narrative reading of how price interacts with
that map.** Practitioners who treat Profile as fully automatic lose the context VPA
preserves; purist VPA readers lose the measurable structure Profile enforces. The
irreducible tension is whether the edge lives in the *number* or the *story*.
