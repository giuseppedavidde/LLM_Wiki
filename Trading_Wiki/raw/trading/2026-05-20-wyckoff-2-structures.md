## Overview

This book by Rubén Villahermosa Chaves combines the logic of the Wyckoff Methodology with the objectivity of Volume Profile and Order Flow analysis. It presents "Wyckoff 2.0" — a modern framework integrating classical Wyckoff principles with contemporary market microstructure tools.

## Part Structure

### Part 1: Advanced Concepts of the Wyckoff Methodology
Covers advanced labeling, price vs. volume analysis, advanced chart types (tick, volume, range charts), accumulation vs. failed distribution, structural failure (weakness and strength), Shortening of Thrust (SOT), sloping structures, and unusual scheme patterns.

### Part 2: Resolving Frequently Asked Questions
Practical guidance on efficient use of lines, context analysis, distinguishing accumulation from distribution, chart analysis methodology from top-down and bottom-up, and handling unclear contexts.

### Part 3: Today's Trading Ecosystem
Overview of modern market participants, electronic markets, algorithmic and HFT trading, OTC markets, dark pools, and the adaptive markets hypothesis.

### Part 4: The Importance of Volume
Comprehensive coverage of Auction Market Theory (AMT), the Law of Supply and Demand, order types, volume analysis tools (order book, tape, footprint, delta), and order flow problems (price divergence, delta divergence).

### Part 5: Volume Profile
Volume Profile as a variant of Market Profile (Steidlmayer, 1985). Composition, profile types, difference between vertical and horizontal volume, profile shapes (P-shape, b-shape), uses for identifying structures, market bias, trend health, VPOC migration, position management calibration, and trading principles with value areas (Trading Range, Reversion, Continuation, Failed Reversion).

### Part 6: Order Flow
Footprint reading methodology (diagonal vs. horizontal reading), imbalances, turning patterns (bullish/bearish), continuation patterns, and fractality.

### Part 7: Wyckoff 2.0
The integrated framework: context analysis (trading range vs. trend), identification of trading zones and levels, scenario planning, and trading strategy combining Wyckoff + Volume Profile + Order Flow.

### Part 8: Case Studies
Real examples applying all concepts across markets (EUR/USD, others).
