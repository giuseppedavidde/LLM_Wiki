# Smartfood: Nutrition and Recipes

## Core Idea
The Smartfood Diet by Eliana Liotta and Lucilla Titta (IEO-certified) applies nutrigenomics to everyday cooking: 30 scientifically-selected foods (20 Longevity + 10 Protective) whose molecules interact with DNA to slow aging, fight overweight, and prevent disease — without sacrificing taste.

## Key Concepts

### Nutrigenomics Foundation
- **Gerontogenes (aging genes)** — p66, Tor: activated by abundant food, drive energy production, fat storage, free radicals, inflammation
- **Longevity genes** — Sirt (Sirt1-7): activated by caloric restriction/fasting, drive tissue repair, anti-inflammatory, lifespan extension
- **Mimicry principle**: certain food molecules (antocianine, capsaicina, curcumina, epigallocatechingallato, fisetina, quercetina, resveratrolo) bind to the same genetic pathways as fasting

### The 30 Smartfoods

**20 Longevity Smartfood** (contain anti-aging molecules):
- Antocianine-rich: arance rosse, cavoli cappuccio rossi, ciliegie, fragole, frutti di bosco, melanzane, mele rosse, patate viola, prugne nere, radicchio rosso, uva nera
- Quercetina-rich: asparagi, capperi, cioccolato fondente ≥70%, cipolle, lattuga
- Fisetina-rich: cachi, fragole, mele
- Others: curcuma (curcumina), peperoncino/paprika (capsaicina), tè verde/nero (epigallocatechingallato), uva (resveratrolo)

**10 Protective Smartfood** (macronutrients, minerals, vitamins):
aglio, cereali integrali e derivati, erbe aromatiche, frutta a guscio, frutta fresca, legumi, olio extravergine d'oliva, oli di semi spremuti a freddo, semi oleosi, verdura

### Cooking Science Principles
- Antocianine: best preserved by steaming, worst by frying (−62%) or boiling (−55%)
- Capsaicina: heat-stable, can be added anytime
- Carotenoidi: bioavailability increases with cooking + oil
- Curcumina: needs piperina (black pepper) and fat for absorption
- Ferro non-eme: pair with vitamin C (lemon, pepper) for absorption
- Fiber: 25-30g/day from veggies, whole grains, legumes, nuts

### Fasting-Mimicking Protocol (IEO)
- One day/week max, adult healthy individuals only
- Allowed: non-starchy vegetables, EVOO, oil seeds, herbs, spices, lemon
- Excluded: animal proteins, legumes, nuts, simple/complex carbs, fruit
- Contraindicated: children, elderly, pregnant, eating disorders, specific pathologies

## Recipe Principles
- All 30 Smartfoods used throughout
- Mediterranean tradition base, open to global ingredients
- Cooking methods preserving vitamin/mineral content
- Visual formula for balanced meals: ½ vegetables, ¼ whole grains, ¼ protein
- Breakfast included as essential meal
