# Toyota Warranty Framework — Overview

Toyota offers a **multi-layer warranty system** that covers new vehicles and extends coverage through regular dealer maintenance. The system is consistent across Europe but with **notable differences between countries** in duration and scope.

## Common Core (EU-wide)

| Layer | Duration | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **New Vehicle Warranty** | 3 years / 100,000 km | Manufacturing defects (mechanical + electrical) |
| **Hybrid System Warranty** | 5 years / 100,000 km | Hybrid battery, inverter, associated ECUs |
| **Paint Warranty** | 3 years | Paint defects |
| **Corrosion Perforation** | 12 years | Rust perforation (body panels) |
| **Roadside Assistance (Eurocare)** | 3 years | Pan-European 24/7 |

## Country-Specific Differences

### 🇮🇹 Italy — [[ToyotaRelaxPlus]]
- **Duration**: up to **15 years** / **250,000 km**
- **Activation**: automatic after each dealer service
- **Coverage per cycle**: 1 year / 15,000 km
- **Battery Care**: [[ToyotaBatteryCare]] — €120/year OR free if always serviced at Toyota dealers
- **Covers**: mechanical + hybrid components (excluding wear items and traction battery by default)

### 🇦🇹 Austria — [[ToyotaRelaxGarantieAT]]
- **Duration**: up to **10 years** / **160,000 km**
- **Activation**: automatic after each dealer inspection (12 or 24 months)
- **Coverage per cycle**: 1 year / 15,000 km
- **BEV Battery**: up to 10 years / 1,000,000 km with battery service check pass
- **Covers**: mechanical + electronic components (excluding wear items)
